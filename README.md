A interactive game of buttons, controllers, and levers to control a ball through a track

## Why i made it:
I took heavy inspiration from a pre-existing game screwball scramble. I always found it fascinating 
how you could use simple mechanisms to guide a ball around of course and so i wanted to attempt to create my own course with a unique structure and mechanisms.

## obstacles: 
- there are 5 in total.
- one is the ball jumper, which you press a button and through the lever mechanism, pushes the platform along with the ball, it features
  little studs on each cylinder to guide the ball
- the second is a screw which is turned through a gear mechanism, the gears are not originally in place and the player must place them effectively
  in order to turn the screw and bring the ball up
- the third is a simple ball drop when it falls through an pin obstacle
- the 4rth is the infamous ball slider where the user must generate enough on the ball by splitting the bars without the ball falling through.
- the 5th is a platform where the user is able to control tilt by moving a controller. Then you win i guess or you design another path or more
  obstacles etc



## How the parts fit:
- this model was designed to be 3d printed in multiple seperate parts assembled together after.
- the models are currently unsliced so you will have to do it yourself
- the main mechanism fits in the bottom and the obstacles will be placed in the upper layer.
- I plan to spit the project into many different parts to 3d print them and combine them into my final project at the end.
